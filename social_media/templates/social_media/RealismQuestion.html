Your experience


Our primary research questions relate to social media. In particular, we are curious how social media-like features affect people's effort, well-being, satisfaction, etc. One mechanism we are particularly interested in is social feedback from your peers.

We suspect that learning about your peers' performance on the intelligence tests can affect how satisfied you are with your own performance. This is perhaps similar to how you might compare your own life to other's lives on, e.g., Instagram.
