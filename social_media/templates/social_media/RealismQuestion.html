Your experience


Our primary research questions relate to social media. In particular, we are curious how social media-like features affect people's effort, well-being, satisfaction, etc. One mechanism we are particularly interested in is social feedback from your peers.

We suspect that learning about your peers' performance at the intelligence test can affect how satisfied you are with your own performance. This is perhaps similar to how you might compare your own life to other's lives on, e.g., Instagram.

Thinking specifically about the social feedback you received during this study (the messages from other participants): how relevant did that feedback feel? What aspects of real social media does it capture or miss?
