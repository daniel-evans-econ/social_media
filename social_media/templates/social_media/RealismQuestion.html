Your experience


Our primary research questions relate to social media. In particular, we are curious how social media-like features affect people's effort, well-being, satisfaction, etc. One mechanism we are particularly interested in is social feedback from your peers.

We suspect that learning about your peers' performance at the cognitive ability task can affect how satisfied you are with your own performance. This is perhaps similar to how you might compare your own life to other's lives on, e.g., Instagram.

How useful do you think the social feedback you received would be for testing this question? What essential aspects of social media does it capture or miss? Please describe your experience with the task, the feedback, and any aspects that felt realistic or unrealistic.
