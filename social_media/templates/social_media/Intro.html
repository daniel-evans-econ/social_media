Task instructions


You will now answer a series of questions. For each question, you will see a collection of eight figures arranged according to a certain pattern. A ninth figure is missing. Your job is to select the figure that best completes the pattern.

You will answer a total of 20 questions across two periods. You will be paid {{ C.PAY_PER_CORRECT }} for each one you answer correctly. You will have up to 90 seconds to answer each question. Questions like these are typically used to measure cognitive ability.

During this study, you will receive messages from other participants. You will also have the opportunity to send messages of your own.

At the end of each period, you will complete a handful of short tasks and make some choices that affect your earnings. There is a potential third period that is strictly optional. Answer each question carefully.
