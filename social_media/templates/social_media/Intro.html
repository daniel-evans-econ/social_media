Task instructions


You will now answer a series of questions. For each question, you will see a collection of eight figures arranged according to a certain pattern. A ninth figure is missing. Your job is to select the figure that best completes the pattern. Questions like these are typically used to measure cognitive ability.

You will answer a total of 20 questions across two periods. You will be paid {{ C.PAY_PER_CORRECT }} for each one you answer correctly.

You will face some of the same tasks, choices, and questions twice.

There is also a possible third period, but it is strictly optional.

Please stay focused and answer each question as carefully as possible.
