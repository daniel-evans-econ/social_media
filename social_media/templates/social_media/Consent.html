What are the purposes of the study?
In this project, we are interested in people's personalities, behavior, and task performance.

What happens to my data?
All participating employees and scientists work in accordance with the provisions of the Data Protection Regulation, the Federal Data Protection Act and the relevant State Data Protection Acts. For this project, we cooperate with research institutions, universities, institutes or laboratories which are not based in the EU. In general, these partners only receive anonymized data. In all other cases, we contractually oblige our partners to process the data as securely as is required in the EU. Those contracts are accepted by the EU for this purpose.

Immediately after collection, your survey data will be stored and statistically analysed. This includes data about when and how often you switched to alternative tabs and windows, though not where you switched to. Results will be reported in a form that does not identify individual participants.

You will be asked to take a photo of yourself using your camera. Your photograph will be blurred locally in your browser, and you will be asked to confirm the blurred photograph before it is transmitted to us. The original, unblurred photograph will not be transmitted to or stored by the research team.

During the study, you will decide whether to send messages to other participants or not. If you choose to send a message, your accompanying photograph may also be shown to them.

We will retain your blurred photograph until the second phase of the experiment is complete, which will be no longer than 6 months after today's collection. It will then be deleted. Access to your blurred photograph will be restricted to authorized members of the research team, subject to the storage arrangements described in this notice.

The legal basis for processing your photograph is your consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR. You may withdraw consent and request deletion by contacting the research team through Prolific. Following withdrawal, we will stop displaying your photograph and delete the stored image. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

Your data will be stored by a technical service provider outside the EU. We have taken all necessary precautions and concluded all contracts which are necessary in order to comply with data protection. Even if the service provider is not located in the EU, your data is secure as we have taken measures and if necessary concluded contracts with the service provider designated for this purpose in the EU.

Which rights do I have?
You have the right to obtain information about the personal data stored about you (Art. 15 GDPR). Should incorrect data be processed, you have the right to rectification (Art. 16 GDPR). When the legal requirements are met, you have the right to request the deletion or restriction of the processing and submit an objection against the processing of your data (Art. 17, 18 and 21 GDPR).

You have the right to complain to the competent data protection authorities.

The consent given here can be withdrawn at any time with effect for the future. However, if your survey data has already been anonymized, it can no longer be associated with you. Therefore we are unable to "remove" your data from the result.

Declaration of Consent
I was sufficiently informed in writing about the scientific study. I have read and understood the information regarding my participation. All questions in this context were satisfactorily discussed and answered. I also had sufficient time to decide whether or not to participate in this study.

I have been informed that my participation is entirely voluntary.

I know that I can withdraw my consent to participate in this study at any time without providing any reason and without having personal disadvantages. If I withdraw my consent to participate in this study, I have the right to request the deletion of my personal data stored within the scope of this study, subject to applicable legal requirements. Data that have already been anonymized can no longer be associated with me.

In case of any publishing by a scientific journal, the data will not indicate who has participated in this study. The applicable data protection laws protect my personal data.
