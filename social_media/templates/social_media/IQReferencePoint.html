Before you begin


In this study, you will answer a total of 45 questions. You will be paid $8 for participating and can earn an additional $0.25 for each question you answer correctly. You will have up to 60 seconds to answer each question.

Your responses to these questions can be used to calculate components of IQ scores. An IQ score is a measure of intelligence used by psychologists. These questions will appear across three periods.

Each period will feature a different type of task, and each task type tests a distinct component of your IQ. In the first period, we test the {{ period_components.0 }} component of your IQ. In the second period, we test the {{ period_components.1 }} component of your IQ. In the third period, we test the {{ period_components.2 }} component of your IQ.

To give you a sense of the scale of IQ scores:

  • The top 2% of people score {{ iq_scale.top_2 }} or higher.
  • The top 16% score {{ iq_scale.top_16 }} or higher.
  • 50% of people score higher than {{ iq_scale.median }}, and 50% score lower.
  • The bottom 16% score {{ iq_scale.bottom_16 }} or lower.
  • The bottom 2% score {{ iq_scale.bottom_2 }} or lower.
