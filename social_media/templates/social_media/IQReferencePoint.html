Before you begin


In this study, you will answer a total of 45 questions. You will be paid $8 for participating and can earn $0.25 for each question you answer correctly. You will have up to 60 seconds to answer each question.

The questions will appear across three periods. Each period will feature a different type of task, and each task type tests a distinct component of your IQ.

In the first period, we test the {{ period_components.0 }} component of your IQ. In the second and third periods, we test the {{ period_components.1 }} and {{ period_components.2 }} components of your IQ, respectively.

Your responses to these questions can be used to calculate IQ scores. An IQ score is a measure of intelligence used by psychologists. To give you a sense of the scale of IQ scores:

  • The top 2% of people score {{ iq_scale.top_2 }} or higher.
  • The top 16% score {{ iq_scale.top_16 }} or higher.
  • 50% of people score higher than {{ iq_scale.median }}, and 50% score lower.
  • The bottom 16% score {{ iq_scale.bottom_16 }} or lower.
  • The bottom 2% score {{ iq_scale.bottom_2 }} or lower.
